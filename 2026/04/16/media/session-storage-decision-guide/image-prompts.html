Image Prompts — Session Storage Decision Guide

April 16, 2026 · 4 min read

Shared Spec (all 5 images)​

  • Dimensions: 1200×630px (blog hero / OG image ratio)
  • Format: PNG, white background
  • Style: Pen-and-ink line drawing with limited watercolor wash
  • Palette (3 colors):
    • Slate blue #4A6FA5 — water, sky, beams
    • Warm sage #7A9A7B — vegetation, wood, buildings
    • Charcoal #3C3C3C — ink lines, figures, geology
  • Detail level: Medium — interesting at full width, clean at mobile
  • No text in any image

1. bellingham-bay-two-lighthouses.png​

Section: Hero (title) Metaphor: Two memory systems that don't connect

Pen-and-ink illustration, 1200×630px, landscape orientation. Bellingham Bay at dusk with two lighthouses on opposite shores. Left lighthouse shines a broad, scattered beam (representing raw session data). Right lighthouse shines a tight, focused beam (representing distilled knowledge). The beams reach toward each other but don't connect — a visible gap over the water. Pacific Northwest treeline silhouette along both shores. 3-color palette: slate blue (#4A6FA5) for water and beams, warm sage (#7A9A7B) for trees, charcoal (#3C3C3C) for ink lines. Light watercolor wash, white background. No text.

2. deception-pass-no-bridge.png​

Section: The Gap I See Metaphor: Raw sessions on one side, distilled knowledge on the other — no bridge

Pen-and-ink illustration, 1200×630px, landscape orientation. Two rocky cliff faces close together, inspired by Deception Pass — no bridge between them. On the left cliff, scattered notebooks, loose papers, and journals (raw sessions). On the right cliff, a neat stack of field guides and folded maps (skills and decisions). A person stands on the left side looking across. 3-color palette: slate blue (#4A6FA5) for sky and water below, warm sage (#7A9A7B) for cliff vegetation, charcoal (#3C3C3C) for ink lines and rocks. Light watercolor wash, white background. No text.

3. bellingham-dock-sorting-catch.png​

Section: Where I Think Session Data Could Improve Squad Metaphor: Reskill as sorting the catch — keeping what's valuable, tossing the rest

Pen-and-ink illustration, 1200×630px, landscape orientation. A fisherman on a wooden dock in Bellingham Bay sorting a morning catch. Fish being separated into labeled wooden crates (keepers) while smaller ones tossed back into the water. Early morning fog over the bay in the background, faint outline of Lummi Island. 3-color palette: slate blue (#4A6FA5) for water and fog, warm sage (#7A9A7B) for dock wood and crates, charcoal (#3C3C3C) for ink lines and figure. Light watercolor wash, white background. No text.

4. nooksack-river-feedback-loop.png​

Section: Ideas I'm Exploring Metaphor: Feedback loop — raw notes flow downstream and return as bound knowledge

Pen-and-ink illustration, 1200×630px, landscape orientation. The Nooksack River curving in a wide loop with Mount Baker visible in the background. Along the upstream portion, small loose papers and notes float on the water's surface. At the bend where the river turns back, the papers transform into small bound books. Pacific Northwest forest (Douglas fir, cedar) lining both banks. 3-color palette: slate blue (#4A6FA5) for water and mountain, warm sage (#7A9A7B) for forest, charcoal (#3C3C3C) for ink lines. Light watercolor wash, white background. No text.

5. whatcom-county-surface-and-aquifer.png​

Section: The Two-Layer Model Metaphor: Visible team knowledge on the surface, invisible platform data underground

Pen-and-ink illustration, 1200×630px, landscape orientation. Cross-section view of Whatcom County. Upper half: Bellingham streets, small buildings, and trees on the surface (visible team knowledge). Lower half: underground aquifer, root systems, and water channels (invisible platform data). A single well shaft connects the two layers, with water visibly flowing between them. Scientific illustration style. 3-color palette: slate blue (#4A6FA5) for water and aquifer, warm sage (#7A9A7B) for surface vegetation and buildings, charcoal (#3C3C3C) for ink lines and geology cross-hatching. Light watercolor wash, white background. No text.
