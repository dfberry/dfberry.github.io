# Image Prompts — Session Storage Decision Guide

## Shared Spec (all 5 images)

- **Dimensions:** 1200×630px (blog hero / OG image ratio)
- **Format:** PNG, white background
- **Style:** Pen-and-ink line drawing with limited watercolor wash
- **Palette (3 colors):**
  - Slate blue `#4A6FA5` — water, sky, beams
  - Warm sage `#7A9A7B` — vegetation, wood, buildings
  - Charcoal `#3C3C3C` — ink lines, figures, geology
- **Detail level:** Medium — interesting at full width, clean at mobile
- **No text in any image**
